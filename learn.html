Movement Library
The Foundational Movements.

Master these nine patterns and you can handle anything a CrossFit WOD throws at you.

01
Air Squat

The foundation of all lower body work. Hips below parallel, knees tracking over toes, chest up. Get this right before loading anything.

Lower Body Beginner
02
Deadlift

The most natural human movement pattern—picking something up. Neutral spine, bar over mid-foot, drive through the floor. Master it light before going heavy.

Posterior Chain Beginner
03
Push-Up

Locked core, full range of motion, no snake necks. The pull-up's less celebrated but equally important counterpart. Scale with knees before breaking form.

Upper Body Beginner
04
Pull-Up

Full extension at the bottom, chin clear of the bar. Start with ring rows and band-assisted variations. Don't kip until you own a strict rep.

Upper Body Intermediate
05
Kettlebell Swing

Hip hinge power, not a squat. Drive with the hips, not the arms. One of the most efficient conditioning movements in existence when done with intent.

Full Body Intermediate
06
Box Jump

Explosive hip extension, land soft and controlled. Start lower than your ego suggests. Step down if you're fatigued—don't let the descent become the injury.

Power Intermediate
07
Hang Power Clean

The gateway Olympic lift. Powerful shrug, fast elbows, catch in a front rack. Teaches explosive hip drive like nothing else in the gym toolkit.

Olympic Advanced
08
Burpee

Everyone's least favorite. Full chest to floor, explosive jump, clap overhead. No skipping the lockout—a half rep is a wasted rep. The benchmark of honest effort.

Conditioning All Levels
09
Double-Under

Jump rope passing twice per jump. Relax the wrists, keep the jump small, stay patient. The most skill-dependent conditioning tool in CrossFit—and worth every frustrating practice session.
